Legal background

Since 28 June 2025, the German Accessibility Strengthening Act (BFSG) requires nearly all B2C providers to make their digital offerings accessible.

A widget never replaces clean code. But a well-built preference layer is a real win for users — and a visible commitment from the operator.

FAQ

Does the widget make my site automatically compliant?

No — and any vendor claiming that is misleading you.

Does it change my HTML or ARIA?

No. The widget never overrides your DOM or ARIA attributes.

How big is it really?

The loader is ≤ 6 KB gzip.

Is there any tracking or cookies?

None.

What does it cost / how is it licensed?
